System for contactless interface control with personalization of gesture commands

Author: Korableva Daniela Mikhailovna

Supervisor: Lukaschik Elena Pavlovna, Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Associate Professor

Relevance of the topic

Traditional input devices — keyboard and mouse — remain the main ones the means of interaction with digital systems, however, they limit the naturalness and accessibility of interfaces. In a number of scenarios, when conducting presentations, work in sterile environments, equipment management on distance — the use of physical input devices is inconvenient or impossible. This leads to an increase in research in the field of contactless interfaces based on hand gesture recognition.

According to scientometric analysis, the number of publications on speech recognition gestures based on convolutional neural networks for the period from 2020 to 2025 demonstrates steady growth with an average annual rate of 8.45 % [2]. This indicates a high academic and the practical significance of the direction.

One of the key problems of existing gesture recognition systems is individual variation of gesture execution. Different users perform the same gesture with different amplitude, speed and shape of the fingers, which reduces recognition accuracy. Research shows that personalized gesture sets that take into account the individual characteristics of the user, they provide higher accuracy and convenience compared to fixed sets [3], [4]. In addition, support for custom personalization is considered important. the direction of development of modern human-machine interfaces [6].

Thus, the relevance of the work is determined by the need to create systems contactless control, able to adapt to individual user preferences without significant costs for retraining models.

Goals and objectives of the work

Objective: to develop a contactless digital interface control system based on static hand gesture recognition, which provides personalization of gesture commands without retraining the model and stable operation in real-world operation.

Tasks:

  • Analysis of modern hand gesture recognition methods and existing approaches to building contactless interface management systems [1], [6].
  • Development of a system architecture that includes hand area detection, gesture classification, unknown gesture detection, and a mechanism personalization of commands [3].
  • Implementation of a web platform for uploading user presentations and settings for individual gesture mapping to control commands.
  • Conducting a comparative analysis of fixed and personalized control modes based on accuracy, false alarms, and time metrics development [4].
  • Collection and analysis of statistical data on user preferences when choosing gestures for various commands.
  • Assessment of the system’s stability under real‑world operating conditions and development of recommendations for its use [5].

Scientific novelty

The scientific novelty of the work is as follows:

  • Reformulation of the problem of detecting unknown gestures in relation to towards the context of personalized management. It is considered unknown not only a gesture that is missing from the training sample, but also a gesture that is recognized by the model, but not assigned by the user to any of them. the team. This approach helps to reduce the number of false positives. without changing the model.
  • Exploring user preferences when choosing gestures for commands Controls: collecting and analyzing data about which gestures users use they choose for specific commands which gestures demonstrate a higher recognition accuracy, and how these preferences correlate with subjective assessment of convenience.
  • Comparative analysis of fixed and personalized modes management by metrics of accuracy, false alarms, delay and development time, which allows quantifying the effect personalization [4].
  • Development of a methodology for assessing the reliability of gesture control systems in in real-world conditions, including a set of metrics and their procedure measurements [5].

Planned practical results

As a result of the work, it is planned to receive:

  • A Django-based web platform for uploading presentations, customizing gesture commands and slide show control using hand gestures.
  • A personalization module that allows the user to choose convenient options for commands [3].
  • A two‑level mechanism for detecting unknown gestures, combining the model’s uncertainty assessment (Shannon entropy, logits margin) and checking whether a gesture belongs to the set of assigned commands.
  • A statistical module for collecting and analyzing data on user... preferences and reliability of the system.
  • Experimental data on the effect of personalization on accuracy and user-friendliness of management.
  • A publication on a research topic (abstracts or an article in a collection conferences).

Review of research and development on the topic

Global level

In the global scientific community, research in the field of gesture recognition They have been actively developing since the early 2010s, covering a wide range of tasks — from static gesture classification to dynamic gesture recognition sequences and sign languages [1], [6]. One of the largest datasets for Static gesture recognition is HaGRID (HAnd Gesture Recognition Image Dataset), containing more than 550,000 images from 37,583 subjects, taken in various lighting and background conditions [1]. Comparative studies show that convolutional-based models neural networks, including ResNet18, YOLO, and MobileNet, achieve at this stage the accuracy range is 98-99% [1], which confirms: when If there is a high-quality dataset, the task of classifying static gestures is it can be solved with high accuracy.

Two approaches to gesture recognition prevail in the scientific literature. The first one relies on classical machine learning methods (SVM, Random Forest) combined with MediaPipe to extract key points. hands [8]. The second uses deep neural networks — ResNet, EfficientNet, YOLO, Vision Transformer [10], [11]. Research shows that deep models, Especially those pre-trained on ImageNet and those pre-trained on target data demonstrate a significant advantage over classical ones. algorithms [1].

A separate area of research is devoted to the personalization of gestures interfaces. Thus, the work of Szedel (2026) presents a personalized A CNN-based wrist and forearm gesture recognition system in which users can define their own gesture sets; validation The accuracy of the system was 0.92, which demonstrates a high potential. a personalized approach [3]. Study Wang et al. (2025) also confirms the effectiveness of personalization: the proposed system with training based on specific user data achieves fast convergence and satisfactory accuracy with less the size of the training sample [4]. The task of detection Unknown gestures (open-set recognition) is actively developing the direction [6].

Research in the field of human-machine interaction highlights the importance of taking into account ergonomic factors when designing gesture devices interfaces [7]. The well-known problem of midi gestures it is fatigue from holding your hands in the air for a long time. Works on accessibility show that simple gestures involving one fingers or one hand are perceived by users as more convenient and require less time to perform [5], [7]. This justifies the need for personalization. as a means of adapting the interface to the physical capabilities of a particular the user.

National level

In Russia, research in the field of gesture recognition and gesture interfaces They are conducted in several scientific centers.

Russian sign language. Significant contribution to the creation of datasets for RYE are provided by the Slovo project, which contains 20,400 videos with 1,000 gestures from 194 native speakers. Systems are being developed based on this dataset. dynamic gesture recognition. The Bukva dataset has also been created for the dactylic alphabet of RYE [9]. However, these datasets They are focused on video and dynamic gestures, which distinguishes them from static ones. the control gestures considered in this work.

Gesture interfaces for robotics. Study [8] presents a system for controlling a manipulator, which includes both static and dynamic gestures. The static part is implemented based on YOLO and MediaPipe with an accuracy of 93.9%, which demonstrates the applicability of static gestures for control tasks. This work can serve as a benchmark for comparative evaluation.

Assistive technologies. Japanese Study (2026) describes a project to develop a non-contact gesture interface for people with motor impairments who use affordable 3D cameras and allowing you to control information devices through movements preserved mobility of body parts [5]. This The direction is close to the motivation of personalization considered in this article. work.

Own results

By the time the work on the abstract was completed, the following results had been obtained:

  • A static gesture recognition model based on ResNet18 architectures [10] using dataset HaGRID [1]. The model demonstrated a high accuracy of classification of target gestures.
  • Implemented a hand area detector based on ResNet18, trained on a dataset of 20,000 images that has achieved 99.75% accuracy. The detector allows you to detect the brush anywhere in the frame, which increases convenience. using the system.
  • A mechanism for detecting unknown gestures based on entropy has been developed Shannon and the logit reserve, as well as the model with the additional "unknown" class on the ASL Alphabet dataset. Further education It allowed us to maintain the accuracy of recognizing target gestures by about 98% and eliminate false positives on gestures that are not included in the training program. the selection.
  • Fully local video stream processing is implemented without saving and data transfer, which ensures user privacy.
  • The system was tested in real conditions using the camera, which has confirmed stable operation regardless of the background and lighting.

Conclusion and prospects

As part of the master's thesis, a contactless communication system is being developed digital interface management based on static data recognition hand gestures and providing personalization of gesture commands. Assumed The scientific novelty is related to the reformulation of the problem of detecting unknowns. gestures in the context of personalized management, as well as with research user preferences and comparative analysis of fixed and personalized modes [3], [4].

The practical significance of the work lies in the creation of a web platform that allows users to control presentations through gestures. , by customizing the set of commands to suit your individual characteristics. It is assumed that personalization will reduce the number of false ones. to increase the user-friendliness without overfitting models [4].

The research prospects include expanding the platform’s functionality (media player control, experimental cursor control mode), as well as adapting the approach for assistive technologies and other application scenarios [5].
